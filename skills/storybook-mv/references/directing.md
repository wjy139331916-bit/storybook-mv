# Director method

For narrative work, write the premise, progression, motif and ending; specify a character relationship only when characters and that relationship belong to the approved concept. Separate confirmed facts from invented incidents. For uncertain reciprocity, show observable responses without silently turning friendliness into confirmed romance. For typography, abstract or non-narrative work, define the changing graphic/sonic state instead of inventing characters or a romance.

For each shot record id, start/end, song section, place/time, framing, objective, visible action, ordered audience reads, camera, lyric treatment and transition. Give actions time to prepare, happen and register. A new background alone is not a new story event.

Reuse a manageable group of places with changing framing, light, occupancy and relationship. Establish a recurring object/place early enough that the ending can recall it. Give a repeated chorus new staging or meaning. Do not use arbitrary scene count as a quality target.

Follow the approved color/emotion grammar. A warm palette may express regret through an empty seat or delayed gesture; other styles may use different devices. Do not impose warmth, romance or a relationship story on a different concept.

Connect shots with meaningful action, matched shapes, camera continuity, foreground occlusion or a returning object. A clean cut is allowed. Constant transition effects can erase stillness.

Choose close-ups, interaction shots and establishing views around information. Keep faces/gestures readable. Do not hold distant figures through several new lyric lines without new acting information.

Review shot contact sheets, strips for important actions/cuts and relevant full-resolution crops. Judge motion with consecutive frames or playable video. Label observations versus artistic judgments.

For an “odd” moment, inspect scale, hierarchy, ground contact, occlusion, timing and camera before polish. Fix common rig bugs once. Keep accepted work and re-render only affected ranges plus adjacent joins.

Use independent agents only when authorized by the user or governing workflow. Assign distinct files and inspect overlapping layers before integration.

Apply the user-stage-review reference linked from SKILL.md: direction acceptance permits design only. Present complete-scope script and actual design frames and wait; then present the pilot and wait; for full MVs present a whole-scope rough animatic and wait before fine production. Existing exact decisions can be reused; no agent self-review supplies user acceptance.
