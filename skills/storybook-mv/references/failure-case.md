# 24-second trial regression case

Source: a user-supplied 1920×1080, 30fps, 24-second clip reviewed on 2026-10-28. Original video, photos and song lyrics are not bundled.

Observed in representative frames and a walking strip:

- Both figures had elongated narrow bodies relative to their heads.
- Opening female lower limbs stretched diagonally far from the torso toward the foreground ground plane.
- Prop exchange used long straight-looking arms with weak elbow/wrist articulation.
- Much of the clip kept one setting while the camera moved closer; framing changes alone added limited acting information.

Source code was not provided. A mixed-coordinate or parent-transform defect is a hypothesis, not a proven source-code diagnosis. This does not establish that all realistic-proportion animation is impossible.

Regression checks:

1. Establish the approved model sheet and consistent local units. The historical project selected 4–5 heads; that is not a global style requirement.
2. Export actual pose coordinates; audit bone lengths, scale, ground and figure bounds over time.
3. Reject unreachable hand targets or change blocking instead of stretching limbs/clothes.
4. Review walk/stop and prop transfer in consecutive frames with correct depth overlap.
5. Give shots observable actions/reads; use camera pushes to reveal information.

Simplification lowers drawing demands without waiving anatomy, contact, timing or storytelling.
